# Early Blight (Alternaria solani)

## Overview
Early blight is a prevalent fungal foliar disease characterized by distinct concentric brown rings ("target-board" or bullseye pattern) that typically begins on senescing lower canopy leaves.

## Etiology
- Caused by *Alternaria solani*, an ascomycete fungus.
- Spores (conidia) survive in infected crop residues and volunteer weeds.
- Heavy dew cycles alternating with warm dry periods facilitate conidial liberation and infection.

## Symptom Characteristics
- Dark brown to black circular lesions with distinct concentric ridges.
- Chlorotic yellow halo encircling expanding necrotic patches.
- Premature leaf defoliation proceeding from ground upward.
