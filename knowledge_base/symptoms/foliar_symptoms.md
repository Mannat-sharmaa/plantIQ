# Foliar Disease Symptoms and Visual Differentiators

## Water-Soaked Lesions
Rapidly expanding, pale to olive-green or dark brown wet patches. In late blight, these lesions appear translucent when backlit, commonly initiating along leaf tips and margins where guttation water droplets collect.

## Target-Board Bullseye Lesions
Concentric rings of alternating dark and light brown necrotized cells typical of *Alternaria solani* (Early Blight). Margins remain well delineated rather than diffusive.

## Chlorotic Halos
Yellowing margin surrounding fungal necrotic spots, caused by diffusible phytotoxins (e.g. alternariol, tentoxin) disrupting chlorophyll pigment synthesis in advance of mycelial invasion.
