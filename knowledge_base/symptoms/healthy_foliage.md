# Physiological Standards for Healthy Foliar Tissue

## Morphological and Histological Integrity
A healthy foliar specimen displays unbroken epidermal wax cuticles, uniform distribution of palisade and spongy mesophyll cells, and consistent chlorophyll a and b absorption spectra. Foliar turgor pressure remains firm without vascular flaccidity or necrotic margin collapse.

## Saliency and Convolutional Verification
Under deep neural convolutional feature extraction and semantic segmentation (U-Net), healthy foliage produces:
- Less than 1% necrotic or chlorotic surface area disruption
- Balanced spatial activation without focal margin clusters
- High multi-class softmax probability for non-diseased baseline

## Proactive Canopy Care
- Maintain 45–60 cm row spacing to guarantee aerodynamic horizontal canopy airflow.
- Inspect foliar abaxial surfaces weekly for phloem-feeding insect vectors.
- Provide balanced nutritional amendments respecting nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium ratios to avoid succulent, disease-susceptible tissue growth.
