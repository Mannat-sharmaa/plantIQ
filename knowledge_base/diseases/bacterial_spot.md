# Bacterial Leaf Spot (Xanthomonas campestris pv. vesicatoria)

## Etiology and Symptomology
Bacterial spot caused by *Xanthomonas* species impacts foliage, stems, and fruit. Initial symptoms manifest on young leaves as small (1-3 mm), circular to angular, dark brown to black water-soaked lesions. As lesions age, centers become dry and paper-like with distinctive yellow halos encircling the necrosis.

## Environmental Epidemiology
The bacteria flourish under warm, humid ambient microclimates (24°C to 30°C) with prolonged foliar wetness exceeding 85% relative humidity. Splash dispersion from rain or overhead sprinkler irrigation rapidly propagates inoculum across the crop canopy.

## Integrated Disease Management (IDM)
1. **Sanitation & Cultural Control**: Prune infected basal foliage using 70% isopropanol sanitized shears. Eliminate overhead sprinkler watering in favor of subsurface drip tubing.
2. **Preventative Bactericides**: Apply fixed copper compounds (e.g. Copper hydroxide, Copper oxychloride) combined with Mancozeb to overcome copper-tolerant bacterial strains.
3. **Biological Treatments**: Foliar applications of *Bacillus subtilis* or bacteriophage preparations during early vegetative stages.
