# Cultural and Agronomic Preventative Practices

## Canopy Microclimate Management
- Maintain spacing of 45-60 cm between rows to ensure horizontal laminar airflow.
- Prune lower 20-30 cm of foliage from indeterminate plants to reduce soil splash inoculation.
- Orient planting rows parallel to prevailing seasonal winds.

## Water Regime Management
- Strictly avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation in late afternoon or night.
- Utilize subsurface drip irrigation or ground emitter lines to maintain zero leaf moisture duration.

## Crop Rotation Guidelines
- Execute minimum 3-year crop rotation away from Solanaceae (tomato, potato, eggplant, pepper).
- Rotate with non-host Poaceae (maize, sorghum) or Fabaceae (legumes) to break oospore and sclerotial cycles.
