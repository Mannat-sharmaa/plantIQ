# Crop Protection and Disease Management Guidelines

## Preventative Bio-controls and Protectants
- Copper oxychloride / Copper hydroxide: Multisite contact protectant forming a barrier against zoospore penetration.
- Mancozeb (Dithiocarbamate): Broad-spectrum foliar protectant. Apply before forecasted rainfall events exceeding 15mm.
- *Bacillus subtilis* / *Trichoderma harzianum*: Bio-fungicide colonizing phyllosphere to antagonize pathogen spore landing.

## Application Principles
- Disinfect pruning tools with 70% isopropanol or 10% sodium hypochlorite between rows.
- Never apply broad-spectrum systemic fungicides repeatedly without rotating FRAC mode-of-action codes to avoid physiological resistance.
- When lesion severity exceeds 40%, rogue out severely infected plants and destroy foliage off-field.
