# Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)

## Overview
Late blight is one of the most destructive plant diseases affecting Solanaceous crops worldwide. Caused by the oomycete *Phytophthora infestans*, it can destroy entire crop canopies within 7 to 10 days under continuous humid conditions.

## Etiology and Infection Biology
- Oomycete zoospores possess biflagellate motility in free water films.
- Penetration occurs through stomatal openings or direct cuticular appressoria within 2 to 3 hours of surface moisture.
- Sporangia are dispersed via wind turbulence and splashing raindrops.

## Critical Environmental Triggers
- Relative humidity: >85% for at least 8 continuous hours.
- Optimal temperature: 18°C to 22°C (sporangial germination) and 12°C to 16°C (zoospore liberation).
- Free moisture or heavy dew on foliage is mandatory for primary cycle initiation.
